## Key Context for the Retrospective
- Verstappen started and finished on pole (grid 1) for Red Bull; Hamilton started 2nd with the Mercedes.
- Verstappen won his first World Championship; the title was decided on the final lap as Hamilton's charge was incorrectly flagged for a track limits infringement directly ahead.
- Verstappen's car #33 was carried as his racing number in 2021; his permanent competition number is 3.
